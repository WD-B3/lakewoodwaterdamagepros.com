We do not promise an arrival time, because crew location and traffic decide it. Same-day response is offered when crews are available. Whether your insurer covers a loss is decided by your policy and your insurer, not by us.

Safety

Water and electricity are dangerous together. If you smell gas, see sparks or are unsure whether it is safe to enter a wet area, leave and call the utility or 911 first. Never wade through water that may touch electrical equipment.
