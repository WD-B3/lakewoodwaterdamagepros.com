Brown rings, a sagging ceiling or a steady drip all point to water above. Here is how to limit it, find the source and repair the damage.

A wet ceiling is an emergency before it is a repair job. Drywall and plaster that soak through get heavy and can fall without warning, so stay out from under a bulge and call (216) 541-8541. The line is open 24/7. Move people and furniture away, put a bucket under any drip and photograph the stain before touching anything.

Ceiling damage can start from a roof leak, an upstairs bathroom, a supply line or an air conditioning drain. This page covers the finished ceiling and what sits above it, including roof leaks, whatever the source. Damage lower on the wall is on the wall and drywall repair page.

When a wet ceiling is an emergency

Call at once if the ceiling is sagging, bubbling or dripping steadily, if water is dripping near a light fixture, or if a stain is growing by the hour. Water that has pooled above drywall can drop in one piece. Poke a small hole at the lowest point of a sagging bulge with something long, standing off to the side, to let the water out in a controlled stream into a bucket.

Light fixtures and wet ceilings

If water is coming through a ceiling light or fan, turn the power off at the panel only if the floor is dry, and do not touch the fixture. Call the utility or 911 if you see sparks or smell burning.

Finding what is above the ceiling

The pattern of the stain often points to the source. A ring that grows slowly after rain suggests the roof or a flashing detail. A stain that appears when someone showers upstairs points to a drain or supply line. A stain near a vent or an attic air handler points to the condensate line.

A moisture meter and thermal camera let a crew see how far the water spread beyond the visible mark, because the stain is only the lowest wet spot. The meter often finds wet insulation and framing well past the stain.

Roof leaks that end in your ceiling

Roof leaks have no page of their own here, because the damage they cause is a wet ceiling. Water enters at the roof, runs along rafters or decking and shows up feet from the actual hole. Missing shingles, failed flashing around a chimney or dormer, and ice backing up under shingles in winter are common causes.

Restoration crews dry and repair the interior, but a roofer has to close the opening. Tarping the roof is a temporary step, and temporary measures to prevent further damage are generally something your insurer expects you to take. Keep receipts for any emergency materials.

How the ceiling is repaired

  1. Water is stopped at the source and any pooled water is released safely.
  2. Wet insulation above the ceiling is removed, and the cavity is dried with air movers.
  3. Sagging or stained drywall is cut out, and plaster is assessed for drying or replacement.
  4. Stain-blocking primer is applied before texture and paint, so the ring does not bleed back through.

Painting over a stain without drying the cavity is the repair that fails. The stain returns, and mold can grow above the paint where no one sees it. A crew should show you moisture readings before the ceiling is closed.

Why this matters in Lakewood's doubles and apartments

Most Lakewood homes were built before 1940, and Census survey figures show a majority of structures with more than one unit and more than half of households renting. That makes ceilings the point where one household's plumbing becomes another's damage. An upstairs unit's leak lands in the apartment below, so documenting the date and the source helps both tenants and landlords sort responsibility.

Questions about ceiling water damage repair in Lakewood

Should I pop a bulging ceiling?

If it is bulging with water and about to fail, relieving it in a controlled way into a bucket is safer than letting it collapse. Stand to the side, wear eye protection and keep light fixtures away.

Can a water-stained ceiling be painted over?

Only after the cavity is confirmed dry and the stain blocked with primer. Painting over wet material hides a problem that tends to return and can grow mold.

Does roof leak damage count as ceiling damage?

The interior damage does. A roofer repairs the roof opening, and the ceiling and insulation are dried and repaired on this page's scope.

How far does ceiling water spread?

Farther than the stain. Water runs along rafters and pools in insulation, so a moisture meter is the only reliable way to find the edge.

Do you handle ceiling water damage repair emergencies at night or on weekends in Lakewood?

Yes. Ceilings fail at night as often as in the day, so the line at (216) 541-8541 is answered 24/7, including weekends. Same-day response is offered when crews are available, and relieving the pooled water safely is the first step.
