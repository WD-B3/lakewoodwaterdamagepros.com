The airport, the land deal and what it means for neighbors

In 2001, Brook Park and Cleveland agreed to a land deal that moved some Brook Park land, including homes and the IX Center, to Cleveland for a Cleveland Hopkins runway extension, with NASA Glenn Research Center coming to Brook Park in return. For a homeowner the practical lesson is that boundaries shift, so check your address and your city's utility and permit offices instead of assuming.
