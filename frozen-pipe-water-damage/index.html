A frozen pipe is a slow-motion emergency. Ice blocks the line and stretches the pipe wall, and the real flooding often begins hours later when the ice melts and water pours from the split. If you see frost on a pipe or a trickle that stops, call (216) 541-8541. The line is open 24/7, and the steps below can limit damage while you wait.

Burst pipe cleanup and frozen pipe cleanup overlap, but the problem here is timing and hidden damage. For a break that is flowing right now with no ice involved, use the burst pipe page. This page is for pipes that froze, cracked and are thawing.

Frozen pipe emergency: do these things first

  • Shut the main water valve before the ice melts, even if nothing has leaked yet.
  • Open the faucets on the frozen line so melting water has a place to go.
  • Do not use a torch or open flame on a pipe. A hair dryer or warm towels are the safer approach.
  • Check ceilings and walls below the frozen run for damp spots, bulges or dripping.

How a frozen pipe becomes water damage

Water expands when it freezes, and the pressure builds in the pipe between the ice plug and the closed faucet. The pipe splits at its weakest point, but the ice holds the water in. When the section thaws, the split opens and the line behaves like any other burst pipe, delivering water into whatever is around it.

That delay is why the damage can show up after you have gone to work or gone to bed. It also means a pipe that looked fine after it thawed may still hold a hairline crack that leaks slowly behind plaster.

Where Lakewood homes are exposed

Lakewood sits on Lake Erie, and the homes are older, with a median build year around 1938 in Census survey data. Pipes on exterior walls, in unheated porches, in the rear kitchen additions many doubles carry and in drafty basement corners are the classic freeze spots. Rental units with vacant apartments can freeze with no one home to notice. For exact freeze-day counts, NOAA publishes local climate normals.

What the cleanup involves

Crews extract water, then use a moisture meter and thermal camera to follow the damp path through the ceiling or wall. Wet insulation is removed, because it holds water against the framing. Plaster and drywall that stayed wet are cut back, and air movers and dehumidifiers dry what remains.

A plumber replaces the split section of pipe and looks for a second crack in the same run. The cleanup is only finished when readings match dry reference points for each material, which a crew can show you on the meter.

Prevention that costs little

Keep thermostats set above freezing in every unit, including the ones you rarely use. Open cabinet doors on exterior walls during cold snaps and let a faucet drip on a line that has frozen before.If you are away, shut the water off at the main and drain the lines.

Questions about frozen pipe water damage in Lakewood

What should I do if a pipe is frozen but not leaking?

Close the main valve, open the faucet on that line and warm the pipe gently with a hair dryer or warm towels. Leaving the main open while the ice melts is how a split turns into a flood.

Why did the pipe leak only after it thawed?

The ice acts as a plug. The pipe splits during freezing, but water cannot escape until the ice melts and the split opens.

Is thawing a pipe myself safe?

Warm air and towels are fine. Never use an open flame, a propane torch or a heat gun that can scorch framing, because house fires from thawing pipes are well documented.

Will a frozen pipe leak again?

It can. The same exposed run may crack again, and a hairline crack can leak slowly. Insulating or relocating the vulnerable section is the long-term fix.
