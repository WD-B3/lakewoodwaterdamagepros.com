About 24/7 Lakewood Water Damage Restoration

A 24/7 water damage line for Lakewood and nearby towns, built around plain scope and price before work starts.

☎ Call (216) 541-8541

24/7 Lakewood Water Damage Restoration is a water damage restoration line for Lakewood, Ohio and the towns around it. The line is answered 24 hours a day, every day, and it connects callers with licensed and insured restoration professionals who handle extraction, drying, cleanup and repair.

How a call works

  1. You call (216) 541-8541 and describe the damage, where the water came from and how long it has been there.
  2. The dispatcher tells you what to do while you wait, such as shutting off the water or the power from a dry spot.
  3. A crew is sent when one is available, and same-day response is offered when crews are free.
  4. At the property the crew assesses the damage, explains the scope and the price and starts work only after that.

What we do not do

We do not promise an arrival time, because traffic and crew location decide it. We do not publish prices, because a price depends on the property. We do not promise that an insurance claim will be approved, because coverage decisions belong to your insurer. We do not offer waterproofing, equipment rental or fire restoration. If your problem needs a different trade, we say so.

Standards we point to

ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published standard for water damage restoration and S520 is the standard for mold remediation. You can ask any company you hire whether its crews work to them. Ask how it documents moisture readings and drying goals, and ask to see the readings when the job is done.

Why Lakewood

Lakewood is a dense lakefront city of older houses, doubles and apartments, with plaster walls, wood floors and basements that make water damage a regular part of life. The City of Lakewood owns the water and sewer mains, and owners are responsible for the pipes on private property. Knowing the local setup helps us tell you quickly whether the problem is yours, the city's or both.
