Lakewood first, then the nearby towns that crews are dispatched to around the clock.

Crews are dispatched from our office at 15000 Detroit Avenue in Lakewood, ZIP 44107. Lakewood is the home city, and three nearby places have their own pages because their housing, geography and local details differ enough to matter. Each page names its ZIP codes and covers local facts that affect how water damage plays out.

Lakewood, ZIP 44107

Lakewood has about 49,000 residents, a lakefront on Lake Erie and a dense mix of older houses, doubles, apartments and storefronts. Neighborhoods include Birdtown, Clifton Park, Downtown Lakewood, the Arts District around the Beck Center for the Arts, Victorian Village, Rockport Square and the Gold Coast along the lake. Start with the homepage or the full list of services.

Nearby towns we serve

Cleveland, OH

Bay Village, OH

Brook Park, OH

Lakewood neighborhoods

Five Lakewood neighborhoods have their own pages: Birdtown in the southeast corner, the Gold Coast along the lake, Clifton Park in the northwest, the West End beside the Rocky River Reservation and Downtown Lakewood around Detroit Avenue and Warren Road. Each page covers the housing and water risks of that part of the city.

Also reachable from Lakewood

Westlake (ZIP 44145), Rocky River and North Olmsted are close to Lakewood, and calls from those places are welcome. They do not have pages of their own here. Call (216) 541-8541 and the dispatcher will tell you what is possible.

Distances are short on a map but traffic and crew location decide how fast a crew arrives, so we do not promise an arrival time. Same-day response is offered when crews are available.
