Privacy Policy

How we handle information from calls and website visits.

☎ Call (216) 541-8541

This policy explains how Lakewood Water Damage Pros handles information when you visit this website or call our line. We keep it short, because we collect very little.

Calls to our line

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we may collect your name, phone number, address and a description of the damage so that we can respond to your request and send a crew.

This website

This site has no forms and does not ask you to type personal information. The site may record basic visit data, such as pages viewed, the type of browser and the general area a visit came from, to understand how the site is used and to keep it working.

How information is used

We use call information to answer your request, to send and coordinate a crew, to follow up on service and to improve our service. We share information with the restoration professionals who respond to your request, and only as needed to do the work.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete information you gave us on a call by phoning (216) 541-8541 or emailing contact@lakewoodwaterdamagepros.com. You can also tell us at the start of a call if you do not want to continue.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always on this page.
